The Jim Rogers Story

A cold email in 2009. A podcast guest in 2026.

In 2009, Yohann sent a cold email to Jim Rogers, co-founder of the Quantum Fund with George Soros. 4,200% returns in a single decade. One of the most sought-after investors alive.

Rogers replied. Most people would have asked for something immediately. Yohann didn't. He kept the conversation going. Year after year. No pitch. No ask. Just genuine connection maintained over time.

Seventeen years later, Jim sat down as the first guest on Pale Blue Nexus. The episode is live.

No agency would have kept that thread alive for 17 years. Most wouldn't last 17 weeks. This is the difference between transactional media and relationship-driven media. We build the second kind. That's why we build shows. It isn't what we sell you. We sell the production that makes a show worth someone's seventeen years.

Please figure out a way to find out what you love the most. Many people never find it. Or even if they find it, they're afraid to do it. Pursue it, pursue it, pursue it.

Questions

Frequently asked.

How does distribution work? Do you need my passwords?

No. You add us as a delegate inside your own accounts. YouTube, your podcast host, and your scheduler all support this natively. You keep every password and can remove access any time.

What if I'd rather post everything myself?

Fine. We deliver finished files, correctly formatted and named for each platform, and you post on your own schedule.

How fast is turnaround?

48 hours per episode as standard, from the moment we have the raw files.

What do I need to record?

A decent mic and a stable connection. We'll set you up on Riverside and give you a short recording checklist. You don't need a studio.

Do you book guests?

No. Studio is production only. You record, we handle everything after.

Can I cancel?

All three tiers are month-to-month. No lock-in.
