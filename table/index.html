The Table is two invite-only rooms in the Toronto to Waterloo corridor. A seated dinner in Toronto. A seated lunch for founders in Waterloo. Two separate rooms, each with its own guest list, built by hand.

What happens

Founders, operators and investors, seated. A short program on where business is going, then the floor is held. No panels, no pitch decks, no badge scanning. Each room is built so the conversations are worth the time.

How people get in

Invitations come from Aloomii. Seats are limited to the size of each room, so the list is built deliberately rather than opened up. Builders who ship consistently at DevKick get first look.
