Common questions

Do I own the website?

Yes, all of it. No lock-in and no monthly fee to me just to keep it online.

Are there running costs?

A domain is about $15 a year, and hosting is free to a few dollars a month. I can set both up and look after them for you if you'd rather not.

What if I don't have content ready?

That's normal. I'll tell you exactly what to send, and I can write the copy for you.

Can you add an online shop?

For a handful of products, yes, with Stripe or PayPal checkout and no monthly subscription. Bigger stores with inventory and shipping rules I quote separately.

How fast is turnaround?

A one-page site is usually about 4 days. Podcast episodes come back in a few days. Rush work is possible, just ask.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Websites can be split into two payments, half up front and half when it goes live.
